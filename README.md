# Miniwo Discord Channel Structure

This document covers categories, channel names, and the purpose of each channel only. Pinned messages and channel content will be handled separately.

## WELCOME TO MINIWO

**Purpose:** Help new members understand Miniwo and find official information.

| Channel | What it is for |
|---|---|
| `What is Miniwo?` | Introduce Miniwo, its core experience, and the most important official links. |
| `Updates!` | Share official product news, development updates, launches, and playtest announcements. |
| `Rules` | Explain the behavior, safety, and content standards expected in the server. |
| `What's on Social Media` | Collect Miniwo's latest official social posts, campaigns, and social media links. |

## COMMUNITY

**Purpose:** Give players one place to talk, ask questions, and share Miniwo-related creations.

| Channel | What it is for |
|---|---|
| `General Chat` | Casual conversation between community members. |
| `Share Your Character` | Share characters brought into Miniwo, character profiles, and relationships. |
| `Share Your Story` | Share Story Cards, memorable moments, scenes, and personal Miniwo stories. |
| `Share Your World` | Share player worlds, builds, locations, themes, and world progress. |
| `Any Ideas or Questions?` | Ask questions, suggest ideas, and discuss possible improvements. |

## PLAYTEST

**Purpose:** Keep technical problems separate from general gameplay feedback.

| Channel | What it is for |
|---|---|
| `Bug Reports` | Report reproducible bugs, errors, crashes, and technical problems. |
| `Playtest Feedback` | Share opinions about gameplay, usability, balance, and the overall playtest experience. |
