# Miniwo Discord Content System

*Research completed: July 29, 2026*

This document contains the writing system and production-ready English copy for Miniwo's approved Discord structure: three categories and eleven channels.

## 1. Research Summary

The strongest communities studied use the same core principles:

- **Keep the server focused.** Discord recommends starting with essential channels because a wall of empty channels overwhelms new members.
- **Treat community as conversation, not a broadcast feed.** Announcements matter, but members stay when they have clear topics to discuss and easy ways to participate.
- **Lead with the next action.** Midjourney routes new members directly to creation and support; Lovart does the same with a short first-creation path.
- **Separate live chat from lasting content.** Text channels work for quick conversation. Forums work better for questions, feedback, bugs, and creations that should remain searchable.
- **Make creative work easy to scan.** Media-focused Forums should use Gallery View; support and feedback Forums should use List View, clear titles, and simple post templates.
- **Write for real people.** Short headings, bullets, white space, and direct instructions are easier to use than long welcome essays.
- **Protect trust.** Good communities set expectations for respectful disagreement, privacy, creator credit, safe DMs, and responsible bug reporting.
- **Create participation loops.** Redbean successfully connects prompts, creation, sharing, replies, and recognition. Miniwo should encourage replies and feedback, not only uploads.

## 2. Miniwo Writing Style Guide

### Voice

- Warm, curious, creative, and direct.
- Friendly without sounding childish.
- Professional without corporate language.
- Community-first: use **you**, **we**, and **let's** naturally.
- Invite participation without pressuring members.

### Grammar

- Use native North American English.
- Use sentence case for headings.
- Use contractions when they sound natural.
- Prefer active voice and concrete verbs.
- Use the Oxford comma.
- Avoid buzzwords, exaggerated promises, and excessive exclamation marks.

### Formatting

- Open with one short sentence that explains the channel.
- Keep paragraphs to one or two sentences.
- Use headings for major sections and bullets for actions.
- Use bold text for labels, not for entire paragraphs.
- Give each message one primary CTA.
- Do not repeat the full server rules in every channel.

### Emoji

- Use one functional emoji in the title or channel identity.
- Do not replace important words with emoji.
- Avoid emoji chains and decorative clutter.
- Never rely on emoji or color alone to communicate meaning.

### Reading level and accessibility

- Target a Grade 7–8 reading level.
- Use familiar words and short sentences.
- Give links descriptive labels when possible.
- Ask members to caption important visual context and mark spoilers.
- Do not require members to disclose age, school, precise location, or other personal information.

### Standard message pattern

Each channel follows the CLEAR structure:

1. **Clear purpose** — why the channel exists.
2. **Lead with action** — what members can do next.
3. **Easy to scan** — short sections and bullets.
4. **Accurate and safe** — verified product language and essential boundaries.
5. **Route clearly** — point members to the correct channel when needed.

## 3. Product Understanding

The channel system is built around Miniwo's official product model, not a generic game-community template.

### Core promise

Miniwo is a **creative life simulation** where players bring characters they love into a living world and create stories that belong to them. The emotional center is personal: familiar faces, new memories, meaningful choices, and worlds shaped by those stories.

### Player experience

The official life loop is **Meet. Choose. Create. Remember.** In practical terms:

1. **Bring characters in** by uploading a photo and welcoming a character into Miniwo.
2. **Meet and interact** with characters and NPCs, make choices, and uncover relationships or story clues.
3. **Create** by gathering, crafting, and building a world of your own.
4. **Remember** by saving meaningful adventures as manga Story Cards.
5. **Share and discover** by posting comic cards, visiting player worlds, and letting each adventure start a conversation.
6. **Grow the world** as stories inspire new building and civilization growth across Magic, Future, and Industrial worlds.

This is a connected loop, not a list of unrelated features: characters create moments; moments become stories; stories become Story Cards; Story Cards inspire building; built worlds create new encounters and stories.

### Community model

Miniwo's strongest community objects are:

- **Characters** — who a player brings into the world.
- **Stories and Story Cards** — what happened and why it mattered.
- **Worlds** — what the player gathered, crafted, built, and grew.
- **Ideas and questions** — how players learn and help shape the experience.
- **Playtest evidence** — what broke and how the experience felt.

The three UGC Forums mirror the official brand line: **Your characters. Your stories. Your world.** This gives every creation one obvious home and turns sharing into conversation rather than an undifferentiated media feed.

### Brand personality

The voice combines cozy warmth with curiosity, discovery, light mischief, and a hint of mystery. It should feel personal and imaginative, while leaving room for Astra, the Final Tide, hidden connections, and the constellation of player worlds. It should not sound childish, overly sentimental, or corporate.

### Approved terminology and claim boundaries

Prefer the official terms **creative life simulation**, **living world**, **Story Cards**, **Workshop**, **player worlds**, **live characters**, **NPCs**, **civilization growth**, **Magic World**, **Future City**, **Industrial World**, and **Astra**.

The supplied product brief describes Miniwo as AI-powered. Public Discord copy should still favor the website's player-facing language—**living world**, **live interaction**, and **characters and NPCs**—unless the team publishes a more specific technical description.

Do not promise persistent memory, unlimited autonomy, specific release dates, platforms, pricing, account features, or any mechanic not confirmed publicly. Use **build your world**, not **build towns**, when speaking generally. The website is the primary source of truth: https://miniwo.ai/

## 4. Structure Review Before Writing

The approved structure should remain at three categories and eleven channels. No additional channel is required at launch.

| Channel | Recommended type | Unique responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| `What is Miniwo?` | Read-only Resource | Product introduction and server navigation |
| `Updates!` | Announcement | Official news, playtests, and event notices |
| `Rules` | Read-only Rules channel | Community-wide behavior and safety standards |
| `What's on Social Media` | Read-only Text | Official Miniwo social posts |
| `General Chat` | Text | Live, casual community conversation |
| `Share Your Character` | Forum, Gallery View | Character-focused creations |
| `Share Your Story` | Forum, Gallery View | Story Cards, scenes, and narrative moments |
| `Share Your World` | Forum, Gallery View | Player worlds, locations, builds, and growth |
| `Any Ideas or Questions?` | Forum, List View | Public questions and ideas |
| `Bug Reports` | Forum, List View | Reproducible technical issues |
| `Playtest Feedback` | Forum, List View | Experience-based playtest feedback |

### Boundaries that prevent duplication

- **General Chat** is for live conversation; completed creations belong in the three Share channels.
- **Share Your Character** focuses on who someone created.
- **Share Your Story** focuses on what happened.
- **Share Your World** focuses on where it happened and what was built.
- **Any Ideas or Questions?** is for public questions and suggestions, not technical bug tracking.
- **Bug Reports** records broken behavior with reproduction steps.
- **Playtest Feedback** records opinions about the experience, pacing, clarity, and fun.
- Introductions can happen naturally in **General Chat**; a separate introductions channel is not necessary yet.
- FAQs, routine public support, and feature ideas can all begin in **Any Ideas or Questions?** at launch. Use tags to separate them as volume grows.
- Events can be promoted through **Updates!** and Discord Scheduled Events; a permanent Events channel is not necessary yet.

Optional naming improvement: `Miniwo on Social Media` is slightly clearer than `What's on Social Media`. The copy below retains the currently approved channel name.

### 🛡️ Rules

**Channel Purpose**
Set clear community-wide standards for behavior, safety, privacy, and sharing.

**Channel Description**

> Read the Miniwo community rules before joining the conversation or sharing your work.

**Welcome Message**
Use the pinned message below as the channel welcome and Rules Screening source.

**Rules or Instructions**

- Keep this channel read-only.
- Keep the Rules Screening version aligned with this message.
- Handle reports and appeals privately.

**Suggested Pinned Message**

```md
# Miniwo community rules 🛡️

Help us keep Miniwo welcoming, creative, and safe for everyone.

## 1. Be kind
Treat people with respect. Disagree with the idea, not the person. Harassment, hate speech, threats, and personal attacks are not allowed.

## 2. Keep it appropriate
Do not post sexual, graphic, hateful, or otherwise unsafe content. Use spoiler formatting for story spoilers or sensitive themes that are allowed.

## 3. Protect privacy and boundaries
Do not share private information—yours or anyone else's. Never pressure someone to share their age, school, location, account details, or private messages. Respect requests to stop contacting someone.

## 4. Share responsibly
Post work you have the right to share. Credit artists, collaborators, and sources. Because Miniwo can bring a character in from a photo, only upload or share a real person's image when you have their permission. Never use someone's image to impersonate, shame, or harass them.

## 5. Keep feedback constructive
Be specific, useful, and respectful. Critique the work, not the creator. Unrequested insults are not feedback.

## 6. No spam, scams, or unsolicited promotion
Do not flood channels, repeat the same post, advertise unrelated projects, send suspicious links, or promote through unsolicited DMs.

## 7. Use the right channel
Keep one topic per Forum post. Use **Bug Reports** for technical problems and **Playtest Feedback** for opinions about the experience.

## 8. Do not publish exploits or confidential test material
Report serious exploits privately to a moderator. Keep unreleased playtest material inside the approved Playtest area.

Moderators may remove content or restrict access when needed to protect the community. Use Discord's reporting tools or contact a moderator privately if something feels unsafe.

**By participating, you agree to follow these rules and Discord's Terms of Service.**
```

**Suggested Emoji:** 🛡️
**CTA:** Accept the rules, then visit General Chat.

## 6. Final Quality Review

- Each channel has one unique responsibility.
- Rules are centralized instead of copied across every channel.
- The three Share channels divide UGC by character, story, and world.
- Questions, bugs, and experience feedback use different templates.
- Every channel ends with one clear action.
- Product language uses Miniwo's official **creative life simulation** positioning and **Your characters. Your stories. Your world.** structure.
- No copy promises release dates, response times, implementation, pricing, or unconfirmed capabilities.
- Public messages do not request sensitive personal or account information.
- Paragraphs are short, headings are consistent, and emoji use is functional.
- All copy uses natural North American English and Discord-ready Markdown.

### Product Accuracy Check

| Official product element | Where the server reflects it | Accuracy boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Bring characters in by uploading a photo | What is Miniwo?, Share Your Character, Rules | Does not claim unsupported character-generation controls; requires permission for real-person images. |
| Characters, choices, and unique stories | What is Miniwo?, General Chat, Share Your Story | Does not promise unlimited autonomy or persistent memory. |
| Manga Story Cards | What is Miniwo?, Share Your Story, General Chat | Described as meaningful adventures members can keep and share. |
| Gather, craft, and build | What is Miniwo?, Share Your World, Playtest Feedback | Uses the official Workshop and world-building language without inventing resource systems. |
| Player worlds, live interaction, and NPCs | General Chat, Ideas and Questions, Bug Reports | Mentions visiting, interacting, and finding story clues without inventing multiplayer rules. |
| Civilization growth | Share Your World | Uses the official Magic World, Future City, and Industrial World labels. |
| Astra and the Final Tide | Product model and General Chat | Treated as official lore references; no additional lore is invented. |

## Strategic Recommendations

1. Publish the read-only welcome content first, then seed each Forum with two or three strong example posts before opening the server more widely.
2. Convert named channel references into clickable Discord mentions after the channels are created.
3. Test every message and link from a normal member account, not only an administrator account.
4. Review this content whenever Miniwo's public feature set, playtest policy, or official links change.
