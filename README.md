# Miniwo Discord Server — Simple Build Plan

## Product Basis

Miniwo is a **creative life simulation** where players bring characters into living worlds, create personal stories through interactions and choices, save memorable moments as manga Story Cards, and collect, craft, and build a world around those stories.

The Discord should therefore focus on **characters, stories, Story Cards, worlds, building, relationships, and Miniwo lore**.

Source: [Miniwo official website](https://miniwo.ai/)

## Channel Responsibilities and Changes

### 🌱 START HERE

| Channel | Responsibility | What to change |
|---|---|---|
| `#start-here` | Explain Miniwo and show members where to begin. | Merge `#welcome` and `#about-miniwo`. Rewrite the product description using the current website. |
| `#rules` | Show short community rules. | Keep it, shorten the copy, and enable Rules Screening. |
| `#announcements` | Hold official Miniwo updates. | Merge `#updates` into it and keep it read-only. |
| `#introductions` | Give new members an easy first post. | Ask what characters, stories, games, or worlds they enjoy. Do not ask for age, exact location, or DMs. |

### 💬 COMMUNITY

The Community category is for **conversation**. Create & Share is for members who have **made something**.

| Channel | Responsibility | What to change |
|---|---|---|
| `#general-chat` | Everyday conversation and casual check-ins. | Rename the current `#general`. Remove the long bot guide and pin one short conversation starter. |
| `#cozy-corner` | Casual talk about games, comics, anime, art, OCs, and creative inspiration. | Rename `#off-topic`. Keep the topic broad but relevant to Miniwo's audience. |
| `#miniwo-chat` | Discuss Miniwo gameplay, new content, favorite moments, and what members want to try. | Create a dedicated product discussion channel so Miniwo conversation is not lost in general chat. |
| `#characters-and-stories` | Talk about characters members would bring into Miniwo, their personalities, memories, relationships, and possible stories. | Create a product-related discussion channel. Conversation belongs here; finished creations belong in the UGC Forums. |
| `#world-and-lore` | Discuss Astra, the Final Tide, world themes, discoveries, and Magic, Future, or Industrial worlds. | Create a dedicated lore and world discussion channel based on the current website. |
| `#ideas-and-questions` | Ask questions and suggest product or community ideas. | Create it as a Forum. Merge useful content from `#faq` and public suggestions into it. |

Recommended `#ideas-and-questions` tags:

`Getting Started` · `Characters` · `Story Cards` · `Workshop` · `Worlds` · `Idea` · `Answered`

### 🎨 CREATE & SHARE — Main UGC Area

| Channel | Responsibility | What to change |
|---|---|---|
| `#character-showcase` | Share imported characters, original characters, profiles, and relationships. | Rename `#character-share`, keep it as a Forum, and simplify its tags. |
| `#story-cards` | Share manga Story Cards and memorable, funny, emotional, or chaotic Miniwo moments. | Create a dedicated Forum around one of the product's main shareable outputs. |
| `#world-showcase` | Share player worlds, locations, world styles, and building progress. | Create a Forum that directly reflects “Every Player Has a Miniwo.” |
| `#workshop-creations` | Share crafted objects, builds, visual concepts, and Workshop creations. | Create a Forum tied to Miniwo's collect, craft, and build loop. |
| `#wip-feedback` | Share unfinished characters, stories, cards, or builds and request feedback. | Create a Forum that encourages useful replies instead of one-way posting. |
| `#featured-creations` | Collect the strongest community creations in one clean gallery. | Create one read-only channel instead of multiple fan-art, screenshot, and hall-of-fame channels. |

Recommended UGC tags:

| Forum | Tags |
|---|---|
| `#character-showcase` | `Imported Character`, `Original Character`, `Profile`, `Relationship`, `Update` |
| `#story-cards` | `Funny`, `Emotional`, `Chaotic`, `Adventure`, `Lore` |
| `#world-showcase` | `Magic`, `Future`, `Industrial`, `Mixed`, `Progress Update` |
| `#workshop-creations` | `Build`, `Crafting`, `Decoration`, `Concept`, `Tutorial` |
| `#wip-feedback` | `Character`, `Story`, `Story Card`, `World`, `Build`, `Need Feedback` |

Use one simple Forum post structure: **What I made / The story behind it / What feedback I want / Image or link.**

### 🧪 PLAYTEST

| Channel | Responsibility | What to change |
|---|---|---|
| `#playtest-news` | Publish test builds, test scope, and known issues. | Rename `#playtest-info` and keep it read-only. |
| `#bug-reports` | Collect reproducible technical problems. | Rename `#beta-bugs`. Keep it as a Forum with `Open`, `Need Info`, `Confirmed`, and `Fixed` tags. |
| `#playtest-feedback` | Collect private-test gameplay and experience feedback. | Rename `#feature-suggestions`. Keep public ideas in `#ideas-and-questions`. |

Only approved playtesters should see this category. The `Playtester` role must not be self-assigned.

### 🛟 HELP

| Channel | Responsibility | What to change |
|---|---|---|
| `#help-and-reporting` | Explain how to get help or privately report safety problems. | Create one read-only resource. Never ask members to post sensitive reports publicly. |

## Simple Roles and Onboarding

- `Member` — normal server access.
- `Playtester` — private Playtest access, assigned by the Miniwo team.
- `Creator` — optional identity role with no extra permissions.

New members should only need to accept the rules, choose their interests, and visit either a Community channel or a UGC Forum.
